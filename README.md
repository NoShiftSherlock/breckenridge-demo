# Breckenridge Distillery storefront on AccelPay (sales demo)

Private sales demo. Uses Breckenridge Distillery's names, marks, product photos and copy from
shop.breckenridgedistillery.com to show their own Shopify store running on AccelPay instead of
Flaviar Checkout. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Breckenridge Distillery, Gopuff, ReserveBar or
Minibar; all marks belong to their owners. Not a store: no payment is taken and no order is placed.
Keep the link unlisted and share it 1:1 only.

Live: https://noshiftsherlock.github.io/breckenridge-demo/

Real: products, prices, images and descriptions (Shopify products.json, 2026-11-17); Gopuff coverage
(146 Gopuff locations stocking Breckenridge, 1,278 delivery ZIPs, which SKUs each location carries,
gopuff_feed 2026-11-17); network retailers carrying the brand by state (2026-11-07).

Illustrative: shipping ($14.99, free over $99 per their live BRECKTHEHALLS promo), Gopuff fee ($4.99),
8% tax, Cask Club 10% discount, blocked states (AK, HI, UT), payment step, confirmation.
